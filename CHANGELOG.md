# Changelog

Each release's changes, newest first. The release workflow takes a release's notes from its
section here.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-08

The first release.

### Playing

- Play Stockfish at eight strengths, from the start or from a position you set up or paste as
  FEN; resign, or leave a game unfinished.
- The analysis panel: Stockfish's best lines, as arrows and numbers, played out on the board.
- Step back through every game you have played, kept on your computer.

### The coach

- Claude coaches you from your own Claude Code, through an MCP server the app runs on your
  computer only: no API key, no account in the app.
- Four ways to be coached: guided, casual, blunder watch and silent.
- The coach draws your options on the board, plays lines out, sets up positions, and can play
  you itself, choosing among Stockfish's best moves.
- Every chess claim the coach makes comes from Stockfish, through the app's tools; a line it
  makes up is checked move by move before you see it.

### Training

- Lichess's puzzles and openings, installed from the Training tab (a 300 MB download, kept as
  the best 200,000 or so puzzles in 40 MB).
- Puzzles by theme and by the piece that wins, with hints; lessons the coach builds from them;
  openings played through on the board.
- A review of each game, with every move marked, once it ends.

### Your progress

- A puzzle rating, the themes you find hardest, and where in your games your mistakes come.
- Weaknesses the coach notes, and puzzles aimed at your weak spots.
- Puzzles you miss, and the worst mistakes in your own games, come back for practice, spaced out
  over days.
